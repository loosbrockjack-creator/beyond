# Week 1 Feedback

## Topic

AI agents and agentic systems

## What worked

- Anthropic explanatory articles were useful.
- The video format was useful.
- Material that focused on mental models and concepts was more valuable than implementation detail.

## What did not work as well

- A significant amount of the OpenAI documentation felt too developer-heavy.
- Some material felt older than ideal for a field moving as quickly as AI.
- Implementation-oriented documentation created more detail than was necessary for the learning goal.

## Preferences going forward

- Strongly prefer material from the last 6 to 12 months when the topic changes quickly.
- Older material is acceptable only when the concept is durable and the resource is still one of the clearest explanations available.
- Favor clear explanatory videos and articles over raw developer documentation when both teach the same idea.
- Technical concepts are welcome when they improve the mental model.
- Avoid code, SDK syntax, API reference detail, and framework configuration unless they are necessary.
- Keep the weekly workload near two hours.
- Focus on knowledge that increases long-term value as AI becomes more capable.

## Long-term objective

Build a durable advantage in AI by becoming strong at:
- understanding what modern AI can do,
- directing capable AI systems,
- problem decomposition,
- system and workflow design,
- context and information design,
- evaluation and verification,
- applying AI to useful real-world problems.

Do not optimize the curriculum around tasks that AI coding systems are likely to automate away.
