# Week 02 Feedback

## Pacing

The learner reported that the previous curriculum took much longer than expected and requested that article times be checked against word counts and average reading speed.

## Apply to future curricula

- Count only assigned sections and identify exactly what to read and skip.
- Show the reading-speed assumption; 200 words per minute is a starting estimate, not a measured personal speed.
- Budget technical comprehension, rereading, and exercises separately from straight reading.
- Keep the total near two hours and use actual completion times to improve later estimates.
