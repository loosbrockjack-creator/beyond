# Current Tutor State

## Current week

Week 2: Working effectively with AI coding agents

## Durable learning preferences

- The learner wants conceptual depth without unnecessary developer detail.
- Explanatory articles and videos have been more effective than raw developer documentation.
- Freshness matters strongly because AI capabilities and workflows change quickly.
- Older material should only be used when the idea is durable and the resource remains unusually valuable.
- The learner wants skills that become more valuable as AI improves, not skills likely to be automated away.
- The target learning workload is about two hours per week.

## Current strategic question

As coding agents become more capable, which human skills remain scarce and valuable?

The curriculum should increasingly emphasize:
- deciding what should be built,
- defining outcomes and constraints,
- decomposing problems,
- supplying the right context,
- evaluating whether the result is actually good,
- connecting AI systems to real business value.
